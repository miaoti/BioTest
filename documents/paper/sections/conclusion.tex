\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\biotest{} is a metamorphic-testing pipeline for textual VCF and
SAM parsers that combines spec-grounded LLM-driven MR mining, a
SUT-agnostic grammar-conforming generator, and a multi-runner
consensus oracle applied uniformly across Java, Python, Rust, and
C++, with per-SUT user effort bounded by the parse-and-emit shim
coverage-guided fuzzers already require. Across six $(\mathrm{SUT,
format})$ configurations it leads on line coverage for five of
six SUTs, stays within a $4.2$\,pp mutation-adequacy deficit on
every text-rich pair while exceeding the strongest baseline on
seqan3/SAM, and detects $10/32$ bugs on a Magma-style real-bug
benchmark where every crash-only input fuzzer detects zero. A
per-SUT coverage-feedback extension on top of the SUT-agnostic
core is a natural direction for recovering the
byte-content-lenient biopython/SAM gap while preserving the
deployment story.
